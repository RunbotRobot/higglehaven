The flower

The bud in the upper left is the flower's head. Tap it: four petals open (Shop, Build, Sell, and the menu). Tap the menu petal and a stem grows down with a leaf for each thing in it; tap your own mode's petal and its stem grows instead; tap another mode's petal and it lights while that place loads, then the flower folds. Everything has a picture and one small word. Names and numbers are examples.

Choices made for you, so you can overrule them

  • A mode's petal does two jobs. The petal of a mode you are not in takes you there (it lights while the place loads, then the flower folds). The petal of the mode you are in opens that mode's stem. Cost: Add item in Build is three taps (the head, Build, Add item); today it is two. If you would sooner the mode's stem opened by itself once the mode has loaded, that is a one-line change.
  • The left-hand leaves. With the head in the upper left there is no room for leaves on its left, so the stem leaves the head, bends right and runs down at 112 px from the edge, with leaves alternating either side. Cost: the stem sits a little to the right of the head. The other way is leaves on the right only, which is simpler to hold but a longer stem for the same six leaves.
  • The menu never changes. The menu stem is the same in Shop, Build and Sell (you, Notices, Friends, Purchases, Settings, Help); what belongs to one place lives in that place's stem. Switch "You are in" above to see the menu does not move.
  • One stem at a time. Opening a stem folds the petals; tapping the head folds the stem. Cost: to change place from a stem you tap the head first.
  • Tips do not need closing. The opening screen's tips are paced for a slow reader, can be switched off in Settings, and can be read again under Help. Cost: a person who reads very slowly may still miss one; a required close would add a tap to every open.
