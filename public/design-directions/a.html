In three sentences

What it is. The interface is made of soft, frosted shapes that condense out of the world's own fog when you need them and thin away when you do not, with a glow where a border would be, and idle controls ghosting down to a faint outline.

What it costs. Text on a translucent surface is only as legible as the view behind it (a bright or busy scene lowers the contrast), ghosted controls are harder for a first-time person to spot, and the blur costs graphics work on older phones.

How a first-time person finds Buy and Settings. Buy is the glowing green button on the card that condenses beside whatever they are looking at, and Settings is the faint gear at the top left, which brightens as soon as a thumb comes near it.
