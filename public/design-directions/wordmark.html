What I need from you

  1. Which of the seven styles to keep (six to eight were proposed), and any you would swap for another look.
  2. Which colour pairs to keep. Ink is the only one that reads over the grass; the others are for the leaf.
  3. Whether the name should stay one colour with a small accent bar (as drawn here) or use the second colour more.
The default look for a lándlet's name is a separate piece of work. No uploaded logos in this version: a choice from a set needs no review beyond the name itself.
