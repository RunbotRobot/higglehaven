Flying up and down

With any of these, flying is level: the view no longer climbs, so you can look straight down at what you fly over. Height changes only with the gesture you pick. Open one on your phone, take off with a double-tap, and fly.

ASlide along the edge

While you fly, slide a thumb up or down along the right edge of the screen: up to rise, down to sink. It works while the other thumb walks.

Costs: a strip about a thumb wide at the right edge (36 px) no longer turns the view while you fly.

Fly with A

BDouble-tap, hold and slide

Double-tap, keep your finger down on the second tap, then slide up to rise or down to sink. A plain double-tap still lands you, or takes you off.

Costs: it takes two taps and a slide, so a small change of height is slower than A.

Fly with B

CPinch

Put two fingers on the same half of the screen. Bring them together to rise and spread them apart to sink. The other thumb can be down walking, or lifted.

Costs: it needs both fingers on one side, so you stop walking or looking for as long as you pinch.
