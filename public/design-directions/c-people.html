The microphone: three ways

The microphone is muted until you turn it on, and then stays on while you walk and talk. Both thumbs are on the sticks while you move, so whatever turns it on or off must be a tap, not something held.

A. A petal, and the bud shows it is live (my recommendation)

Open the bud, tap Microphone (it says Microphone on when live). While it is live the bud has a green ring, and a brighter ring while you are speaking. Nothing else stands on the world.

Cost: two taps to turn it on or off. That is rare: it stays on for a whole conversation, and the bud shows it without a word. Standing cost: nothing when muted, a ring on the bud when live (the bud is there anyway).

B. Hold the bud to talk

Press and hold the bud to speak; let go and it is muted again, like a walkie-talkie.

Cost: you cannot move and talk at once (a third finger is needed), and the bud already means “open the menu” on a tap, so a slow tap could turn the mic on by accident. It also takes away the hands-free conversation people have today. Standing cost: nothing.

C. A small standing microphone by the bud

One small round button beside the bud, the only standing control in the corner besides the bud itself. One tap turns the mic on or off, and its colour says which.

Cost: one more thing always on the world, about 0.9% of the screen (today’s microphone button is the same size). Benefit: one tap, and you can see the state without opening anything. It is the one case where a standing control can be argued for; A is the case that it cannot.

Things that do not change

  • A block stays silent: the blocked person sees nothing different, and the blocker is simply not in their Nearby list. Mutes stay undetectable and conduct reports stay invisible to the reported person. None of the new leaves shows any of it.
  • Chat and the microphone still wait for the same verification they wait for today.
  • The assistant leaf keeps what the earlier fixes gave it: one thread with its history, her name once, above the keyboard, and on top of any other leaf.

Then, in slices

  1. The mark, and Notices and Friends as leaves. The dot on the bud for a notice or a friend request; the old badges and the two old modals go.
  2. The assistant as a leaf, the pill goes, and the bud wears her name while a conversation is minimised. Includes where she is standing and an offer to walk you there (not a spawn point).
  3. Chat as a leaf, and the microphone (A, B or C once chosen). The Chat button, the microphone button and the voice list go.
