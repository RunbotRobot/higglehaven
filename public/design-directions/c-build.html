What becomes what

Today in BuildIn the bud
Shop, Build, Sell, gear, compassThe bud. The compass goes (the world has the sun and the horizon). The bud wears a small “Building” mark.
+ Add Item and the 220 px catalog panelAdd item petal: a short strip of recent items and a search line; the full catalog is one tap behind “All items”.
Undo, RedoOne petal with both halves. Only drawn once there is something to undo or redo.
“Ground” pill and the level menuLevels petal: a leaf that says which level you are on, with Build above and Dig below.
Measure, Multi-Select (and its brush row), PasteTools petal. While a tool is on, the bud wears its name (“Measuring”, “Selecting”). Paste is listed only after a Copy.
Move, Rotate, Trim, Snap on the selection toolbarHandles on the item itself (see below). Snap becomes a setting in the item card’s More.
Copy, Delete, Save Bundle, Bundle, Unbundle, EditThe card that blooms beside the item (or beside the selection). Delete stays immediate and can be undone, as today.

Moving you, or moving the item

One finger drag on empty worldWalk (the same as in Shop, leaf 2).
One finger drag that starts on the selected itemMove the item along the ground; it stops at the lándlet’s edge and says so once.
Drag a corner handleResize.
Drag the ring, or twist two fingers on the itemRotate about the upright axis. The ring is what finds the other two axes (its card has them).
Second finger drag, anywhereLook, always.
Tap an item / tap empty worldSelect it and bloom its card / put the selection away.
Hold on empty world, then dragPaint a selection (in Tools > Selecting; this is today’s brush).

What does not fit, said plainly

  • Claiming a lándlet is a map you pick a plot on; it cannot be a small card. It stays one large leaf (the only large one in Build).
  • Text entry (search, naming a bundle, a sign’s words) needs the phone keyboard; those leaves sit above the keyboard, and Save Bundle keeps its own modal because it is a destructive-looking, named step.
  • Undo and Redo have no gesture: three-finger swipes belong to the phone. They stay a petal, one tap from anywhere.
  • The brush size is a slider, which needs a card. It blooms beside the selection while Selecting is on.
  • Precise placement (nudging by a small amount) has no gesture here yet. Today it is the arrow handles; the plan keeps arrows inside the card’s More until someone proposes better.

Then, in slices

  1. Enter, Add item, place one thing. The Build petal set, the catalog strip, dragging to place. The old Build toolbar goes for exactly these tools.
  2. Select, handles, the card. Move, resize and rotate on the item; Copy, Delete, More; the old selection toolbar goes.
  3. Undo/Redo and Levels. The pair petal, the Levels leaf, the “Ground” pill goes.
  4. Tools. Measure, Selecting with its brush, Paste.
  5. Bundles. Bundle, Save, Unbundle, Edit in the card, keeping their warnings and the live-template confirmation.
  6. Claim, Backup Chain, Community Sign and Calendar. The claim leaf and the card’s More.
