What it is

The whole interface folds into one small bud in the corner of the screen; tapping it unfolds a few petals (Settings, Friends, Chat, Purchases, Build and Sell), and everything else is done by touching the world itself: drag to walk, drag with a second finger to look, double-tap to fly, tap a product to have its card bloom beside it.

What it costs

It is the quietest of the three, but it gives up the visible joysticks and fly button for gestures that nobody can see until they have been taught once, and a petal's label is only readable while the bud is open, so tools get harder to find at a glance.

How a first-time person finds Buy and Settings

They tap the one visible thing on the screen, the bud, which pulses and carries the word Menu until it has been opened once, and they tap a product itself, where a yellow Buy petal blooms beside its name and price.
