In three sentences

What it is. Nothing floats over the world: a product's name, price, seller and Buy are on a small sign planted beside it, Settings is a board you walk up to, and where you are is painted on the ground.

What it costs. A sign can only be read when you are near it and facing it, so a person who just wants Settings has to go and find the board, small type on a distant sign is hard to read on a phone, and a crowded landlet could hide signs behind products.

How a first-time person finds Buy and Settings. Buy is the green tab on the sign next to whatever they walked up to, and Settings is the notice board that stands by the place they arrive, with a signpost pointing back to it from anywhere.
