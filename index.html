Refund Policy

When you return a purchase, you'll get back 99% of what you paid — not the full 100%.

Here's why: every purchase pays a small commission to the builder whose lándlet hosted the product. That commission is real money the builder can withdraw right away, and it's part of what makes building on higglehaven worthwhile. If a refund clawed back 100% of the purchase, someone could exploit that — buy their own item, cash out the builder's commission, then refund the purchase for a full 100% back, pocketing the commission for free. Keeping back that last 1% closes the loophole without asking builders to give up their earnings or shoppers to wait for their money.

In short: builders are foundational to the community we're building, and this is the simplest way we found to protect their earnings while still standing behind every purchase with a real refund.

Some sellers choose not to accept returns on certain products — that's noted on the product before you buy.
